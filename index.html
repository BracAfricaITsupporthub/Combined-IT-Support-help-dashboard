Meeting rooms

TV connections & adapters

All meeting rooms are equipped with a wall-mounted display and an HDMI input panel on the table. Adapters are available for USB-C and Mini DisplayPort laptops.

  1. Plug the HDMI cable into your laptop (use an adapter if needed)

Printers

How to connect & use office printers

Step 1 — Add the printer

  1. Open Settings → Bluetooth & devices → Printers (Windows) or System Settings → Printers (Mac)
  2. Click Add printer and wait for the network scan
  3. Select the printer by name (see list below)
  4. Windows will auto-install the driver; Mac may prompt to download — accept
  5. Print a test page to confirm

Scanning to email

  1. Load your document face-down on the scanner glass or in the ADF
  2. Press Scan on the printer panel
  3. Select Scan to Email
  4. Enter your email address (or select from the address book)
  5. Choose PDF or JPEG, then press Start
  6. Check your inbox — scans typically arrive within 60 seconds

Laptop issues

Common laptop issues & what to do

Slow performance

  1. Close unused browser tabs and applications
  2. Restart the laptop (not just sleep — full restart)
  3. Check disk space: if less than 10 GB free, clear temp files
  4. If still slow after restart, raise a ticket with "slow laptop" in the subject

Frozen / unresponsive

  1. Wait 30 seconds — sometimes a process finishes in the background
  2. Press Ctrl + Shift + Esc to open Task Manager
  3. End any process showing "Not Responding"
  4. If completely frozen, hold the power button for 10 seconds to force shutdown
  5. Turn back on — if the issue recurs, raise a ticket

Battery draining fast

  1. Check for runaway processes in Task Manager (CPU > 20%)
  2. Reduce screen brightness to 50–70%
  3. Disconnect unused USB peripherals
  4. If battery life is under 2 hours on light use, request a battery check via ticket

Blue screen (BSOD)

  1. Note the error code at the bottom of the blue screen
  2. Restart — most BSODs are one-time events
  3. If it happens twice in the same day, raise a P1 ticket with the error code
  4. Do not attempt to reinstall Windows yourself
Never attempt hardware repairs yourself — all laptop servicing is done by IT.

Biometrics

Biometric access & attendance

The office uses fingerprints or staff badges for door entry.

First-time enrollment

  1. Visit IT with your employee ID badge
  2. IT will register your fingerprint and badge at the enrollment terminal
  3. Scan each finger 3 times until the system confirms capture
  4. Test access at the nearest biometric door before leaving

Troubleshooting

  • "Fingerprint not recognized": Clean your finger and the sensor. Try a different finger. If repeated failures, visit IT for re-enrollment.
  • Door won't open after successful scan: The lock may have a 3-second delay — wait briefly. If still stuck, use the intercom or call security.

Security

Password change & 2FA activation

Change your password

  1. Press Ctrl + Alt + Delete and select Change a password
  2. Enter your current password, then your new password twice
  3. Password requirements: min 12 characters, upper + lower + number + symbol
  4. Wait 5 minutes for the change to propagate to all systems
  5. Update saved passwords in your browser and mobile apps
Passwords expire every 90 days.

Activate 2FA / MFA

  1. Sign in to your Google/Gmail account at Google My Account – Security
  2. Under “How you sign in to Google”, select 2-Step Verification
  3. Select Get Started and re-enter your account password
  4. Choose a second step, such as Google Prompt, text/voice code or an authenticator app
  5. Follow the on-screen instructions to verify your device or phone number, then turn it on

Phishing — how to spot it

  • Unexpected emails asking you to "verify your account" or "reset password" — always check the sender address
  • Urgency or threats (for example, "Your account will be locked in 24 hours") — pause and verify the request through an official channel
  • Links that don't match the displayed text — hover before you click
  • Attachments you were not expecting — do not open. Report suspicious messages to IT at itsupport.africa@brac.net or through the approved IT support channel.
